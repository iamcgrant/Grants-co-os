# Client App

Installable PWA experience branded as **Grants & Co** (not the internal stack).

## Routes

| Route | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `/portal` | Home — journey, next action, credit snapshot, notifications |
| `/portal/credit` | My Credit — bureau scores, models, history chart |
| `/portal/payments` | Invoices, pay securely, history, refunds |
| `/portal/documents` | Secure document access |
| `/pay/[invoiceNumber]` | Grants Pay checkout |
